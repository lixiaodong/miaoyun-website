MiaoYan AI Keyboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Effective date: October 25, 2026

This is the English translation of the privacy policy for the version of MiaoYan AI Keyboard downloaded from our official website (miaoyanzhixin.com / miaoyan.co, including the Diệu Ngôn edition). The Chinese version at /privacy.html prevails in case of any inconsistency. The version distributed on Google Play has its own policy at miaoyan.co/privacy-en.html.

Shenzhen Chainfun Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us") understands how important your personal information is to you. As required by law, we take appropriate security measures to keep your personal information safe and under control.

0. Scope of service and where data is stored (please read first)

  • All cloud data is stored on compliant servers in mainland China. We currently do not operate servers outside mainland China.
  • This service is not offered to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland. If you are located in one of these regions, please do not use this service.
  • If you use this service outside mainland China, you understand and agree that your information will be transferred to servers in mainland China for processing.

About "Automatically recognize the chat partner" (Accessibility service): this feature is off by default, is only offered in the website versions, and must be turned on by you under system "Settings → Accessibility". Once on, the app only reads the name at the top of chat screens in the chat apps listed above, to match it on the device against chat partners you created. It does not read, store or upload any chat content, and it never taps or types anything on your behalf. You can turn it off at any time in the same place, and it stops immediately.

About "Reply directions": when the keyboard receives the other person's message, it automatically offers a few reply directions (such as agree, politely decline, change the subject) for you to choose from. To generate them, the other person's message, excerpts of the profile you organized for that chat partner, and the recent conversation are sent to our servers in mainland China and handled in the same way as the existing "AI reply"; you can turn this off under "Settings → Reply directions". Location (optional): only after you tap "Use my location" and agree in the system prompt will the keyboard read your current location once, when the other person asks where you are or about a meal; it does not track location in the background. Coordinates and address text stay on your phone and are filled into the reply by your phone; they are not uploaded to our servers. Our servers only know whether a location is available. You can revoke the location permission in system settings at any time.

2. How we use information

  • To provide core features such as AI smart replies, text polishing, screen scanning and speech-to-text
  • To count AI usage in order to provide membership services
  • To improve and optimize our AI results

3. About the AI algorithms

Our AI reply and image-recognition features are provided by calling compliant third-party large-model services deployed in mainland China (DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud Qwen, Moonshot AI Kimi); speech-to-text is provided by Alibaba Cloud Model Studio real-time speech recognition (with iFLYTEK speech recognition as a fallback when it is unavailable; both are called from our server). The chat context and screen-scan screenshots you submit are first de-identified on our server and then sent to the model services above to generate replies; they are not sent to model services outside mainland China. Voice recordings are used only for transcription; audio is not stored. We do not use your input data to train models.

All AI-generated replies are suggestions only; you alone decide what to send.

5. Your rights

  • Access: you can view saved contact information and conversation history in the app
  • Correction: you can edit contact information at any time
  • Deletion: you can clear chat history, delete contacts, or permanently delete your account and all associated data via "Settings → Account security → Delete account"
  • Withdrawing consent: you can turn off clipboard and microphone permissions in system settings at any time

6. Data security

We protect your data with SSL/TLS encrypted transmission, database access control and other technical measures. All cloud data is stored on compliant servers in mainland China.

7. Protection of minors

This service is intended mainly for adults. If you are under 14, please use it under the guidance of a parent or guardian.
