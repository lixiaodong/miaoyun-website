  • All cloud data is stored on servers located in mainland China. We do not currently operate servers outside mainland China.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features: AI reply suggestions, text polishing, screen recognition and speech-to-text
  • To count AI usage for membership services
  • To improve and optimise the quality of our AI output

3. About the AI

Our AI reply and image-recognition features are delivered through FoxAI, a gateway operated by us that calls third-party large language model services (including OpenAI). Speech-to-text is implemented via the iFlytek MSC SDK. Conversation context and screen captures you provide are de-identified server-side before being forwarded through that gateway solely to generate a reply. Voice recordings are used only for transcription and no audio is retained. We do not use your input data to train models.

All AI-generated content is a suggestion only. The decision whether to send it is entirely yours.

5. Your Rights

  • Access — you can view saved contacts and conversation records inside the App
  • Correction — you can edit contact information at any time
  • Deletion — you can clear chat history, delete contacts, or permanently delete your account and all associated data via Settings → Account Security → Delete Account
  • Withdraw consent — you can revoke clipboard and microphone permissions at any time in your device settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in section 8. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Data Security

We protect your data using SSL/TLS encryption in transit and database access controls. All cloud data is stored on compliant servers located in mainland China. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The App is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you are under 14, please use the App only under the guidance of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
