MiaoYan AI Keyboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026
Effective: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the MiaoYan AI Keyboard app distributed on Google Play (package com.aiinput.keyboard). The version of the app distributed in mainland China has its own policy, in Chinese, at /privacy.html.

Shenzhen Chainfun Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") operates the MiaoYan AI Keyboard app ("the App"). This policy explains what information the App collects, why, where it goes, how long we keep it and how you can delete it.

0. Summary

  • You can use the App without an account (as a guest). Signing in only needs an email address and a one-time code — no password.
  • Text you choose to send for an AI reply is processed by OpenAI on our behalf. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your data to train AI models.
  • Our servers for this version of the App are located in Singapore. One exception: the email that delivers your sign-in code is sent through our mail server in mainland China (see section 4).
  • When you hold the space bar to talk, your voice is streamed to our servers and transcribed in real time by OpenAI, then lightly punctuated by AI. Recordings are not stored — the audio is discarded as soon as it is transcribed.
  • The App does not access your location, your phone's contact list, your SMS, or your advertising ID.
  • You can delete your account and data in the App at any time: Settings → App settings → Delete account.
  • The App is not offered to, and is not intended for, users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland. If you are located there, please do not use the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App's features: AI reply suggestions, polishing, reply directions, screen recognition and personalisation
  • To run your account, apply free and paid quotas and prevent abuse
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems

We do not use your messages, screenshots or documents to train AI models, and we do not use them for advertising.

3. About the AI

AI replies, polishing, reply directions, screen recognition and speech-to-text are provided by OpenAI (United States) through its API, as our service provider. OpenAI processes the data we send only to return a result to us. AI output is only a suggestion — nothing is sent to anyone unless you choose to send it.

5. Deleting Your Data

  • Delete your account in the App: Settings → App settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your contacts, AI reply history, conversation summaries, documents, profiles, personas, scenes, memories and usage records, and removes your email address from your account record. If you later sign in with the same email, you get a brand-new account.
  • Delete individual items at any time: deleting a contact also deletes its conversation summary; deleting a document deletes its text.
  • Without the App: see miaoyan.co/delete-account.html or email us (section 8).
  • What we keep: records of payments you made are kept as required by accounting law; they contain no chat content.

6. Data Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS); the App does not allow unencrypted connections. AI reply history, conversation summaries and memories are additionally encrypted in our database. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Age Requirement (18+)

The App is only for people aged 18 or over and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
