MiaoYan AI Keyboard — Delete Your Account and Data

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data in the MiaoYan AI Keyboard app (Android package com.aiinput.keyboard, also published in Chinese as 妙言知心), developed by Shenzhen Chainfun Technology Co., Ltd. (深圳市链方科技有限公司).

1. Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open MiaoYan and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to Settings (设置) → App settings (通用设置).
  3. Tap Delete account (注销账号) at the bottom of the page.
  4. Confirm in the dialog. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

2. Delete only part of your data, without deleting your account

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove content. Inside the app you can delete individually:

  • Uploaded documents — Document library (文档库): delete one document, or Delete all.
  • Contact / customer records — Customers (客户): delete any record.
  • Custom personas and scenes — Personas (人设) / Scenes (场景).
  • Sales and relationship profiles — Business profile (销售大脑 / 恋爱大脑): clear the stored profile.

3. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email jetty@ichainfun.com from the email address registered to the account, or state the phone number registered to the account, with the subject "Delete my MiaoYan account". We will complete the deletion within 15 working days and reply to confirm.

4. What is deleted

When an account is deleted, the following are permanently erased from our servers and cannot be recovered:

  • AI conversation logs and text-optimisation logs
  • Screen-recognition logs and AI usage logs
  • Contact / customer records you created
  • Custom personas and scenes
  • Uploaded documents and their derived vector embeddings
  • Sales and relationship profiles ("business brain" / "romance brain") and relationship graphs
  • Reply statistics, daily usage counters, payment order records and referral records

5. What is retained, and for how long

  • The account record itself (your phone number and/or email address) is retained in a deactivated state rather than erased from the database. It is excluded from all queries and the account can no longer be used. Its only purpose is to let you restore the same account if you later sign in again with the same phone number or email, and to prevent repeated abuse of new-user free trials. If you want this record erased as well, say so in your email request under section 3 and we will erase it.
  • Server access logs that may contain the request are kept only for security and troubleshooting and are rotated automatically. They contain no app content.
  • Transaction and invoicing records are retained for the minimum period required by the accounting and tax laws of the People's Republic of China, separately from your app content.
  • Guest (not signed-in) usage records are automatically deleted 90 days after they are created.

妙言知心 —— 注销账号与删除数据

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 25 日

本页说明如何删除 妙言知心 / MiaoYan AI Keyboard（Android 包名 com.aiinput.keyboard）的账号及相关数据。 应用由深圳市链方科技有限公司开发运营。

一、在 App 内注销账号

  1. 打开妙言知心并确保已登录。
  2. 进入设置 → 通用设置。
  3. 点击页面下方的注销账号。
  4. 在弹窗中确认。注销立即生效，且不可恢复。

二、只删除部分数据，不注销账号

不必注销整个账号也可以删除内容。App 内可单独删除：

  • 文档库：删除单个文档，或「全部删除」。
  • 客户：删除任意客户记录。
  • 人设 / 场景：删除自建人设与场景。
  • 销售大脑 / 恋爱大脑：清空已存档案与关系图谱。

三、通过邮件申请删除

若无法进入 App，请用账号绑定的邮箱发信至 jetty@ichainfun.com， 或在信中写明账号绑定的手机号，主题填「注销妙言知心账号」。我们将在 15 个工作日内完成删除并回复确认。

四、会被删除的数据

注销后，以下数据将从服务器永久删除且不可恢复：

  • AI 对话记录、文本优化记录
  • 扫屏识别日志、AI 用量日志
  • 你创建的联系人 / 客户资料
  • 自定义人设与场景
  • 上传的文档及其向量化数据
  • 销售大脑 / 恋爱大脑档案与关系图谱
  • 回复统计、每日用量计数、支付订单记录、邀请记录

五、会被保留的数据及保留期限

  • 账号本身（手机号 / 邮箱）会被标记为已注销并保留，而不是从数据库中抹除。该记录已被排除在所有业务查询之外，账号无法继续使用。 保留它的唯一目的，是让你日后用同一手机号或邮箱登录时可以恢复原账号，并防止反复领取新用户试用。 如果你希望连这条记录一并抹除，请在第三节的邮件申请中注明，我们将为你彻底删除。
  • 服务器访问日志仅用于安全与故障排查，按滚动策略自动轮转覆盖，其中不含应用内容数据。
  • 交易与开票记录按中华人民共和国会计与税务法规要求的最短期限保留，并与应用内容数据分离存放。
  • 未登录（游客）用量记录在创建 90 天后自动删除。
